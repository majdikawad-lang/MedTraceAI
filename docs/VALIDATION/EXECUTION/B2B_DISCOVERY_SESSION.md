# MedTrace AI — B2B Clinic Discovery Session Protocol

## Protocol Specifications
- **Target Audience:** Clinic Medical Directors, Practice Managers, Health System Innovation Officers, and Outpatient Operations Executives.
- **Session Duration:** 30–45 Minutes.
- **Goal:** Explore operational pain, buyer personas, EHR integration requirements, security compliance needs, and commercial SaaS models without pitching or proposing pricing upfront.

---

## 9-Step Business Discovery Questions

### 1. Operational Problem Discovery
- *"In your outpatient practice or clinic system, what is the biggest operational or workflow problem surrounding pre-visit patient history collection?"*
- *"How much time do your primary care physicians or medical assistants currently spend compiling historical records before consultations?"*

### 2. Existing Process & Workarounds
- *"How is pre-visit longitudinal record review handled in your clinic today?"*
- *"What workarounds or tools (e.g., patient intake questionnaires, manual chart prep) do you currently use?"*
- *"What are the biggest limitations or costs of your current process?"*

### 3. Solution Value Drivers
- *"Looking at MedTrace AI's automated Doctor Brief generation and biomarker trend graphing, what specific capability—if any—would create real operational value for your practice?"*
- *"What would make a tool like this genuinely worth paying for from a clinic budget perspective?"*

### 4. Buyer Persona & Budget Ownership
- *"In your organization, who would typically own the budget for a pre-visit clinical workflow tool?"*
  - *Probe neutrally: Practice Management, Chief Medical Officer, IT / Health Informatics, or Patient Experience?*

### 5. Deployment & Implementation Requirements
- *"What would be required operationally for your clinic to deploy a pre-visit summary platform?"*
- *"How do your staff members typically adopt new clinical software?"*

### 6. Mandatory Security & Compliance Controls
- *"What specific security, privacy, or compliance certifications (e.g., HIPAA BAAs, SOC 2 Type II, third-party pen tests) would be mandatory before signing an agreement?"*

### 7. EHR & System Integration Expectations
- *"What clinical software systems (e.g., Epic, Cerner, AthenaHealth, eClinicalWorks) would MedTrace AI need to integrate with for your clinic to evaluate it?"*

### 8. Clinical Pilot Prerequisites
- *"What specific evidence or performance metrics would you need to see before agreeing to a 60-day clinical pilot in 2–3 of your provider offices?"*

### 9. SaaS Pricing Model Discovery
*Rule: Ask this ONLY after understanding their current workflow and perceived value.*
- *"What pricing model would make the most operational sense for your organization?"*
  - *Probe neutrally: Per-clinician monthly SaaS subscription, per-generated-Doctor-Brief transaction fee, or annual clinic system license?*
- *"What cost range would align with your existing clinical software benchmarks?"*
