# MedTrace AI

> **Your Medical Second Brain.**  
> **Understand the Patient. Trace the Evidence.**

MedTrace AI is a clinical intelligence platform designed for **B2B healthcare organizations**.

It helps clinicians understand patient information by connecting clinical context, evidence, timelines, and AI-assisted reasoning into a structured clinical workspace.

MedTrace AI is designed to keep the **clinician in control**. The system provides evidence-based clinical intelligence and reasoning support; it does not replace clinical judgment or present itself as an autonomous diagnostic authority.
